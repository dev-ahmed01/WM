## 2. Operating model (general merchandise / e-commerce / 3PL)
[general knowledge] Goods flow: inbound (appointment, unload, check against PO/ASN, receive) → storage (putaway, location control, rotation) → inventory control (cycle counts, adjustments) → order fulfilment (pick, pack) → outbound (stage, load, carrier handover) → returns (inspect, disposition). Cross-cutting: health & safety, equipment, quality holds, shift operations, IT/WMS support.

## 3. Roles and daily activity
[general knowledge, corroborated in part by UK standard and LSCI packer curriculum] Receiving associate; picker; packer; dispatch associate; inventory associate; quality inspector; material-handling worker (forklift/pallet jack — licensed/trained); returns associate; maintenance associate; general associate; shift supervisor; warehouse manager. UK "supply chain warehouse operative" standard describes moving, packaging and stock-taking duties and lists picker/store person titles [sourced].

## 4. Common mistakes and exceptions (design drivers)
[general knowledge] Short/over/damaged receipts; wrong item or quantity picked; mis-scanned or unreadable labels; wrong location putaway; mixed stock in a bin; missing inventory; expired stock; wrong or missing carrier documents; late dispatch; return with missing parts; scanner/printer/WMS/network faults. Each maps to an exception SOP and an escalation path. These shape the 85-SOP library and the evaluation set.

## 5. Safety references
[sourced] OSHA treats powered industrial trucks, ergonomics, material handling, hazardous chemicals, slips/trips/falls and robotics as principal warehouse hazards; forklift struck-by and tip-over are leading forklift hazards; do not exceed rack load capacity; heavier loads on lower/middle shelves; brake and position forklifts before battery change/charge; propane trucks create carbon-monoxide risk. Relevant US standards named: 29 CFR 1910 Subpart N, 1910.178. **These are US references, not Indian compliance.**

Design consequence: safety SOPs must (a) tell untrained workers to stop and escalate, (b) never give repair/hazard-handling instructions beyond reporting and isolation awareness, (c) point to the site's emergency channel.

## 6. India (kept separate)
- OSH Code 2020 sets a standard 8-hour day / 48-hour week [sourced], but sources disagree on whether it is in force and the Factories Act 1948 may still apply pending rules [open].
- Whether a given warehouse is under the Factories Act, a state Shops & Establishments Act or the Code depends on the premises and state [general knowledge; **verify with counsel**].
- Women night-shift rules, overtime, leave and registration are state/code-specific [open].
- WorkMate stance: demo SOPs carry generic, clearly labelled content; any statutory limits (hours, overtime) are configuration owned by the client, not hard-coded legal claims.

## 7. Training and documentation practice
[general knowledge] Induction + role training, SOP acknowledgement, toolbox talks, forklift licensing, shift-start briefings, handover logs, incident/near-miss registers, goods-receipt notes, damage reports, dispatch documents. LSCI packer curriculum is 292 hours total at NSQF 3 [sourced]. WorkMate supports acknowledgement and handover, not certification.
