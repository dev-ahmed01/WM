# User Personas

All personas are **hypotheses** from general warehouse knowledge, to be validated by interviews. Names are fictional.

## Employee personas
| Persona | Job profile | Language / tech comfort | Main needs | Pain points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ravi, 24 | Picker | Hinglish, voice-first, low literacy in English forms | "Galat item pick ho gaya, ab kya karun?", short-pick handling | Waits for supervisor; scanner trouble |
| Sunita, 31 | Packer | Hindi, basic smartphone | Fragile-item and label rules | Unsure of packaging selection |
| Imran, 28 | Receiving associate | English/Hinglish, comfortable with scanner | PO check, shortage/damage reporting | Discrepancy paperwork |
| Deepak, 35 | Material handling | Hindi, licensed operator | Forklift defect reporting, stop-work | Pressure to continue with faulty gear |
| Meena, 27 | Inventory associate | English, comfortable | Cycle counts, adjustments needing approval | Variance rules |

## Administrator
**Kavita, Knowledge/Training Admin** — owns SOP upload, review, versions, employee onboarding. Needs: fast document-to-draft, see what staff couldn't get answered, safe publishing. Pain: SOPs in PDFs/Word; no feedback on use.

## Manager
**Rahul, Shift/Warehouse Manager** — needs open exceptions, escalations, handover status, SOP gaps, resolution times, within his warehouse only. Pain: constant interruptions; no view of recurring problems. Explicit non-goal: using AI chat for individual performance scoring.

## Supervisor (support actor)
Receives escalations and handovers; may be an employee with extended scope.
